Light Lab

Nine benches. An aperture cuts a beam. A lens bends it to a focus. A prism splits it into a spectrum. A thin film turns thickness into color. A polarizer keeps one wiggle of the wave. Two slits turn overlap into bright and dark bars. A mirror returns each ray at the angle it arrived. Air scatters the blue aside, and a low sun turns red. Red, green, and blue lamps add to white. Cyan, magenta, and yellow inks overlap toward black.
