Cut the beam

Mullions occlude the beam into shafts. The lens bends what gets through into the pool. Dust in the beam is scatter. Desktop draws the shafts with an occlusion-map radial blur on one WebGL context. A narrow screen, a coarse pointer, or ?safe=1 skip that pass and use additive shaft quads. ?still=1 and reduced motion hold the scene.
