Two reflections meet

A soap film. Watch which colors grow stronger, and which cancel.

Light reflects from the front of the film. It also crosses the film, reflects from the back, and comes out. The back path is longer. That extra length is the path difference. If the waves line up, that color gets stronger. That is constructive interference. If the waves land out of step, that color cancels. That is destructive interference. Thickness sets the path difference, so thickness picks the hue. A steeper view shortens the path inside the film. The bands shift. The front reflection flips. The back one does not. Near zero thickness, every color cancels. The film looks dark. Gravity pulls the liquid down, so the top is thinner and goes dark first.
